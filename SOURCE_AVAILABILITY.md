# Source availability and limits of reproduction

This deposit audits publicly displayed numbers and documents their sources. The published preprint, Table 3, carbon-fraction discussion and historical emissions chart were recoverable. The pre-tabulation aggregation workbook, operational class lookup and classified numerical rasters could not be located among the records available for this analysis. Their absence does not establish whether they were lost, retained elsewhere or superseded. No claim of a confirmed raster erratum is made.

The code reproduces the printed entries, density ratios, support factors, cross-interval membership, alternative display-error models and conditional historical shares. Truncation reproduces all five percentages and both historical endpoint labels, but the actual formatting operation has not been recovered. A hidden-baseline rounding explanation for the historical endpoints is also recorded.

The proposed class-2/3 interval transposition and common normalization are diagnostics. A pure metadata relabelling preserves the displayed total; a numerical aggregation correction may not. Determining the cause requires the original class key, area calculation and aggregation output. A common-mask comparison with Feldpausch and Baccini likewise requires the original spatial surfaces rather than their global totals alone.

No matched census dataset or carbon-fate observation has been analysed here. The exact identities and labelled synthetic cases specify testable conditions; their randomized tests are algebraic. Independently measured gains, mortality, regrowth, size compartments and release histories are required for an empirical correction.

The Ecofys chart is available through the cited archive mirror. A publisher-hosted copy or specific Wayback snapshot was not verified; no invented archive timestamp is provided. Figure reuse considerations are in `RIGHTS.md`.
